## Selection and tie-break

The minimum score is 2, shared by upgrade path and compatibility breadth. Select **compatibility breadth** because the observed gap is in the phase's immediate playability-evidence surface: `gpu_motion` has an existing pinned frame-120 oracle and fixture test, but the compatibility ledger's live producer substitutes frame 60. Recording a fresh result for that exact supported predicate makes the report more informative for users and the upcoming playability phases. This adds one fixture observation only; it does not make a title, profile or retail-playability claim.

The upgrade-path gap affects future maintenance, but vulnerability alerts are enabled, no alerts are open, most active pins are current and the only specific patch candidate has exact digests for a normal reviewed update. A grouped updater remains a valid future option, but the evidence shows less immediate user impact than leaving a runnable, pinned compatibility predicate out of the current report. No change was selected before this complete score table.

## Mandatory work kept separate

The quality producer bounds and missing exact outputs, existing CI todo dispositions and audited documentation freshness remain mandatory phase work. They are not counted as the selected improvement. Dependabot auto-merge stays disabled. Compatibility counts and isolated fixture checks do not establish integrated playability; all unknown rows and the experimental libretro status remain unchanged.
