## Flagged assumptions

- **Unresolved edge probe (FRAME-04, unclassified).** The spec-less edge probe could not classify FRAME-04, so its
  row stays unresolved rather than auto-resolved. Assumption carried by this plan and plan 01-07: the requirement's
  edges are the negative controls above (each `abi.*` gate and `core.no_host_clock` fails on its fixture) and the
  byte limits `docs.budgets` applies, read as sizes in bytes with "under 40 KB" and "under 16 KB" meaning strictly
  below 40,960 and 16,384. The verifier should confirm this reading or flag it.
- **Compile and run checks, not gates (ROADMAP SC5).** `abi.header.c17`, `abi.header.cxx17` and `abi.api_v1` carry
  the `abi.` prefix but are compile and run checks, not gates in SC5's sense: each builds or runs a program against
  the real header and fails by a compile error or a non-zero exit of that program, so there is no input a fixture
  could plant to make a passing gate wrongly pass, and they have no `.self_test`. SC5's "each ABI gate fails its
  negative control" covers `abi.symbols`, `abi.globals`, `abi.undefined`, `abi.api_version` and
  `core.no_host_clock`, each paired with its `.self_test` above. The SUMMARY records this classification.
