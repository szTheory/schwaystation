<objective>
Make `schwaystation-run` the frame oracle: per-region and per-frame pinned hashes, the `hashes.txt` file every
lane uploads, the PNG writer whose output RetroArch's screenshot is compared with, the known-answer test for the
shared 15-bit converter, and the usage contract.

the runner and the adapter share the converter, D-06's known-answer test is the only thing that can catch a wrong
conversion rule.

</objective>
