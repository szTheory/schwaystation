### Amendment judgement (plan 03-20)

- **GTE-02, chain-looping "terminating by the guard" to "yielding at the guard while the frame still ends" (D-12).** Justified.
  I fetched the upstream log at the pinned commit (`dma/chain-looping/psx.log`): the single and double self-referencing chains
  report `finished = false, irq = false` after 25,632 and 25,640 ticks, so the hardware never completes or aborts the list; a
  "terminating" requirement would contradict the only hardware evidence. The amended text is the stronger, testable claim:
  `dma.list.self_ref` asserts the 1000h-node yield, BUSY kept, no flag, no interrupt, and a frame that ends; `fixtures.dma_chain`
  pins `list_single` and `list_double` to 00000000 `hw` from log lines 9 and 11 (I checked each cited line).
- **GTE-02, per-word costs "24 or 40" to "24, or 40 selected by the CD-ROM device end in phase 6" (D-11).** Justified, with one
  advisory. The 40-clock cost depends on the CD-ROM delay setting, which only exists once the CD-ROM device end is built
  (phase 6); channel 3 is pinned at the 24-clock BIOS default and `dma.costs.per_channel` pins it. The deferral is in
  03-CONTEXT's deferred list and `docs/hardware/dma.md` (which states plainly that it is unmodelled and that the engine bound
  already allows 10000h words at 40). The advisory: no todo or DISC-01 wording carries it forward (see frontmatter
  `advisory_notes`). That is a tracking improvement, not a weakened phase 3 claim.
- **GTE-03, "judged against PX.12 and PX.21" to ranges derived from the frame constants, every phase-3 row `doc` (D-22).**
  Justified. I fetched `timers/psx.log` at the pin: Timer 2 sysclk/8 reads 71,407 to 71,412 per frame, about 0.9 % above the
  70,775 an NTSC frame gives (405350378496/715909 clocks / 8), so that console cannot back an NTSC `hw` row; and D-39 records
  that the PX.21 "112,0xx to 112,5xx" figure is a 16-bit wrap artefact. The amended wording is narrower in source (`doc`, not
  `hw`) but honest, and keeps the per-profile judge and the exact 715909/451584 ratio; the dot-clock and hblank pointer to GAME-02
  stays. No row is labelled `hw` that the evidence cannot support.
- **GTE-01, "`mailbox@30` holds at most 30 records".** Justified: `tests/fixtures/src/mailbox.inc` caps the record count at 30
  (byte 0Ah, 240 bytes of records); the earlier "64" in the preparation file was impossible. The fixture holds 26 `hw` rows.
- **ROADMAP criterion 2** carries the same yield wording; the criteria 1 and 3 are unchanged. No criterion lost a check.
