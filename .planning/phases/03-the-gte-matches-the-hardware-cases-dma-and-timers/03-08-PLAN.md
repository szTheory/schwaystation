  truths:
    - "The DMA engine is a scheduler device that is data-atomic and time-evented: a unit moves its words at once through `schway__bus_ram_word` and sets `busy_until = now + cost`; `schway__dma_next` reports `busy_until` while a unit is in flight, and the event at it clears BUSY, sets the DICR flag and raises the I_STAT bit 3 edge, then the next unit or a CPU window follows (D-09)"
    - "Engine step contract (stated once here; plans 03-10 and 03-14 build on it): one `schway__dma_event(inst, now)` call runs the due units in order, finishing each unit whose `busy_until <= now` (BUSY clear, DICR flag, I_STAT edge as above) and starting the next after its CPU window, until no unit is due at `now` or until the 1000h-node yield (plan 03-14); a yield returns with the channel's CHCR BUSY (bit 24) still set, no flag and no interrupt; every return, the yield included, leaves `schway__dma_next(inst)` strictly greater than `now`, so `schway__sched_run` and `cpu_run` always make progress (D-09, D-12, D-23)"
    - "OTC (DMA6) starts only with CHCR bits 24 and 28 and its DPCR enable, ignores direction, step, sync and chopping, writes BCR words (0 means 10000h) from MADR downward, each the address of the previous entry and the last FFFFFFh, wraps MADR at FFFFFCh, costs one cycle per word on the 17/16 RAM row (4,096 words take 4,352 cycles), and reads back CHCR 00000002h, or 50000002h when bits 28 and 30 were written (D-11, D-14)"
    - "A bus access that waits on the bus (a RAM, I/O, EXP or BIOS read, an instruction-fetch miss, or the fifth queued store) while `now < busy_until` of a transferring unit sets `stall_until = busy_until` and ends the block, so the CPU freezes until the unit is done; scratchpad, I-cache hits, COP0 and the GTE never stall, and a channel only waiting for DREQ never stalls; a CHCR read right after a long OTC already sees completion (D-10, D-14, D-31a)"
    - "DPCR resets to 07654321h; a lower priority value wins and a tie goes to the higher channel number; the CPU priority field has no effect; a channel whose enable bit is clear does not start, and a DPCR write re-arbitrates (D-13; nugget tests/dma-priority/RESULTS.md at 9db6d88d23c4)"
    - "DICR bit 31 is not stored: it reads as bit 15 OR (bit 23 AND any enabled flag); a flag is set only when its enable and bit 23 allow it (psx-spx, not the ares behaviour); writing 1 to a flag clears it; I_STAT bit 3 is raised only on a 0-to-1 change of bit 31; nugget's DICR values 84840000h, 84800000h, 04000000h and 84008000h are pinned with the edge and no-edge cases (D-13)"
    - "A DMA word address past the RAM window drops the word with no first fault and no RAM write; DICR bit 15 on this path is unverified on hardware, so it is neither built nor asserted (D-14)"
    - "Stores are buffered: with a unit transferring and the write queue empty, the first four back-to-back RAM stores do not stall and the fifth does (the full-queue branch of `cpu_wq_push` from plan 03-03 reaches `schway__dma_bus_access`); five scratchpad stores never stall (D-10)"
    - "OTC ignores the direction, step, sync and chopping bits: written with all of them set, it builds the same table, at the same cost, as the plain case, and CHCR still reads back 00000002h; BCR = 0 transfers 10000h words (D-14)"
